# Roadmap — Navin Journal

**Current phase (2026-11-14):** Stage 5, integrating the real encrypted Library/Reader/Writing Studio in fictional-only APK 02. Stages 1–2 research and blueprint are done; Stage 3 visual direction is approved but physical-device signoff is open. Stage 4 encryption, transactional recovery, independent-key clean reinstall, simulated low-storage and read-only orphan assessment have passed their cited engineering test gates; physical power-loss, media scale and audit remain open. **Do not enable private journal input** until the full security/recovery release gate is met.

Studied Day One, Journey, Diarium, Apple Journal, Daylio, Grid Diary, stoic., Rosebud, Reflectly and Penzu; identified writing, long-term archiving, ownership, privacy and cross-device reliability as core opportunities. Product research is not a live user study.

## Stage 2 — Product blueprint [DONE: initial specification]

Product promise, P0/P1/P2 boundary, proposed navigation, data-trust contract and technical candidates documented. Security and usability validation still outstanding.
